Nightmares

These are your run of the mill evil entities, They typically relate to fears and or other phenomenon 

that only really affects one type of entity, typically relating towards humans. Some examples of 

such nightmares can be imps, demons, entities based on sins, entities such as the ones in the 

backrooms and more. Typically these entities are extremely weak but the higher end ones can be 

very problematic and can sometimes match the strength of True Nightmares.

  

True Nightmares

True Nightmares are created in two ways, one is through natural birth, and the other is when an 

entity is conditioned to so much trauma that they become infatuated by their nature essentially 

becoming one. These entities are a lot rarer than regular nightmares due to there only being one 

of that nature and their nature must affect most entities in existence or have a big enough 

relevance on intelligent entities. What these natures are tend to influence how the True Nightmare 

acts. Examples of said natures are Agony ,Insanity ,Progress, Perspective, Joy, etc. 

  

Eternal Nightmares

These entities are by far the rarest as only one has ever been discovered, being the backrooms as 

the Eternal  Nightmare of liminality. As far as I know it seems like they have some way of affecting 

the reality surrounding them. Not much else is known about them or how they come to be, this 

will certainly require more research to understand.
