Base knowledge

  

Tni (true nightmare of insanity) is an entity meant to represent one’s own insanity and is a part of everyone in existence. Though while they are constantly with you, you can’t see them until you are around 70% - 60% sanity. Due to them being a part of everyone, they are able to manipulate entities in a way that it would be hard to even consider it manipulation. Typically they look like a cloaked humanoid entity with a mask, the mask has a hole for an eye and the top left of the mask is completely broken revealing a purple glowing eye. Though this is their base form they can shapeshift to manipulate their victims more, however this happens very rarely. Their most defining trait is that they aren't really there; they are just a manifestation of your insanity and thus cannot interact physically with anyone.

  

Abilities

  

Shapeshifting - Tni can morph into any entity he wants (such as tails, 2011x, ect) , this form is almost indiscernible from the real version with one key difference being their left eye will always glow purple. This is present in almost all entities but is even more indiscernible should the morph already have purple eyes or no eyes at all.  
  
Memory - while not really an ability, due to them being a part of everyone’s psyche they have all of an entity's collective memory. This means that they know everything about an entity from their earliest memories to the present. This also means that they not only know the best way to break you, but also can’t be tricked as once you think about it they already know it.

  

Hallucinations - similar to entities like childhood has passed they can create extremely realistic hallucinations. Unlike childhood has passed however, they do not need to rely on something like nostalgia to get their way. Rather they just need to get the entity to lose enough sanity in order to cause said hallucinations. Should an entity lose too much sanity they can be trapped in these hallucinations similar to a coma and they can be harmed by them. Essentially rotting their brain from extreme trauma.
