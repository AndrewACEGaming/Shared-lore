
# Experiments so far

## Experiment on mysterious crystal #1


This log will go over some of the experiments I have tested on the crystal that I have recently acquired. This crystal seems to be the key to producing a new type of energy that may help me reach perfection.

  

The color of the crystal appears to be a cyan blue and floats in the air without suspenders, this may allude to it being magnetic like nickel.

  
  

Experiment 1: hardness

First test: copper

Result:no scratches, appears to be higher than 3.5 on the hardness scale

  

Second test: steel

Result: no scratches, higher than 6.5 on hardness scale

  

Third test: Diamond

Result:still no scratches, passes type 1 civilization material hardness

  

Final test: neutronium

Result: slight scratching, however seems to instantly revert said scratch in seconds

  

Experiment 2: X-ray Fluorescence (XRF) 

Results: shows practically nothing, strange since this means its unusually soft similar to muscle when in reality it should be dense like any other crystal

  

Experiment  3: acids

First test: Hydrofluoric acid (HF)

Result: crystal appears to be composed of no silicates 

  

Second test: Sulfuric acid

Result: nothing, seems it wasn’t made with any metals in mind

  

Third test: Aqua Regias acid (Royal water)

Results:still nothing, there were no noble metals used in the creation of this crystal

  

Final test: Nitric acid

Result: somehow ignited the acid even though there was supposedly no combustible materials, regardless it has partially destroyed my machines but it's nothing I can’t fix, still ill make sure not to put it in anything that is somewhat combustible next time.

  

Conclusion: nothing to write home about, seems like the crystal was made from a material that alludes even to me, further info will be needed before I can produce a replica of the crystal for energy conversion. Perhaps there is someone else in that universe that has the info I need? This crystal frustrates me as much as it fascinates, alongside further info gathering more experimentation shall take place later on.


## Experiments on mysterious crystal #2
**

This round of experiments will more so focus on what the crystal can do and how it affects certain components. The crystal will first be tested with regular components such as different elements and electronics before getting into more anomalous objects. It should be noted that after a few days the crystal seems to have dimmed in color, perhaps due to it being so far from its universe.

  

Test 1#: energy transformation

Experiment 1#: crystal was connected to a machine outputting the same voltage as an average car battery

Result: crystal seemed to have regained its original color during this and created more of the chaotic energy that was first detected, unfortunately it overloaded the machine's fuses and violently exploded.

  

Experiment 2#: crystal was this time connected to a machine that outputted the same energy as 1kg of uranium-235 or 24 million kilowatts of power. This time the machine was hooked up to a battery more able to handle sudden rises of energy.

Result: Machine was able to consistently keep up with the energy creation of the crystal and store it in the battery, however the energy quickly dissipated back into regular energy after approximately 1.46 seconds.

  

Conclusion of Test 1#: Crystal shows promise of being able to be used for perfection, however it appears I still need to find a way to store the energy.

  

Test 2#: Anomalous objects

Experiment 1#: crystal was submerged in a vat of liquid sadness from a stressed human  
Result: seemed to transform the liquid sadness into liquid joy surprisingly. This could mean the crystal is somehow connected to emotions.

  

Experiment 2#: crystal is submerged in a vat of almond water

Result: nothing

  

Experiment 3#: crystal submerged in a vat of liquid pain

Result: transformed the liquid pain back into almond water, this could be useful for replenishing my supply of almond water

  

Experiment 4: crystal was put in contact with voidstone, acquired during trade with the M.E.G

Result: the indestructible and unshapeable material suddenly was malleable for a short period of time before becoming rigid once more, this is a massive breakthrough and should allow for far better machines and storage units than ever before 

  

Conclusion of Test 2#: the crystal is able to change objects to a more safe version and or make objects behave in the opposite way. As useful as this is, there is still much I do not know about these crystals and there has been no progress on recreating said crystal.
