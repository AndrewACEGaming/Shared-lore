**This will most likely take place some time after Daedlus’s first interaction with Sonic and co.

  
  

Cream was walking down a path next to a forest at dusk. She was on her way to the grocery store

in order to get some butter for her mom. While she was on her way she spotted a strange Chao,

its skin was midnight blue, its eyes were white with black pupils save for its left eye which glowed a

violet purple. “Chao~” said the Chao, swaying in the air back and forth.  “Hi Chao, are you lost?”

said Cream, the Chao’s bulb turned into a question mark seemingly confused. The Chao then went

into the forest , “Wait Chao don’t go into the forest you’ll get more lost!” Cream said a bit

worryingly. Cream went into the forest chasing after the Chao but it seemed to always stay just out

of her reach. She went deeper and deeper into the forest as dusk turned to night. At last the Chao

stopped at what seemed to be a house and entered. “I hope mother will understand if I tell her

what happened” She said. Cream now looking at the house noticed some features, it was heavily

worn out, what little paint was there had faded and the roof had multiple holes in it, all the

windows were broken and it was a miracle that the house hadn’t collapsed. Cream enters the

house and finds the Chao sleeping next to a book, “is this your home?” Cream says but the Chao

gives no response. Cream picks up the book, the title reads “ The three birds of the black forest”. 

She opens the book and begins to read, “ Once upon a time. Three happy birds lived in a warm 

lush forest. The birds had no names, though the creatures of the forest would call them big bird, 

long bird, and small bird. Everyone lived freely and happily together in the peaceful forest. The 

birds wanted the forest to be safe more than anyone else. That way the creatures from the outside 

could visit and have fun.”. She suddenly realizes the passage of time and closes the book, “What 

am I doing? I need to get the butter for my mother!”. She looks at the Chao , still asleep "I'll come 

visit you again ok?” She says before rushing out of the house to get what her mother wants.
